The Midnight Library

Matt Haig

Release Date :

August 13, 2020

Written language :

English

Description:

Between life and death there is a library.

When Nora Seed finds herself in the Midnight Library, she has a chance to make things right.
Up until now, her life has been full of misery and regret. She feels she has let everyone down, including herself.
But things are about to change.

The books in the Midnight Library enable Nora to live as if she had done things differently...

Categories:

Fiction, Fantasy, Contemporary, Magical Realism, Science Fiction.
